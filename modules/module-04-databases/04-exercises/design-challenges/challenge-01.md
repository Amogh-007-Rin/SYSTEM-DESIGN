# Design Challenge 01: Twitter Schema Design

**Difficulty:** Medium

## Prompt

Design the database schema for a Twitter-like social network: tweets, follows, likes, and retweets. Justify your SQL vs. NoSQL choice for each entity — it's acceptable (and often correct) to make different choices for different entities in the same system.

## What to Produce

1. Functional requirements you're designing against (post, follow, like, retweet, view timeline)
2. Schema for each core entity, with primary keys, foreign keys, and the indexes you'd add and why
3. An explicit SQL vs. NoSQL justification — don't just pick one without reasoning
4. How you'd model the timeline read path differently from the source-of-truth schema, and why
5. At least 2 trade-offs
